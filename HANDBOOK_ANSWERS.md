## What is Caprae's mission?

Caprae runs private equity as an operating discipline not a financial-engineering
exercise. The firm treats an acquisition as the start of a seven-year value-creation
cycle rather than the finish line SaaS and MaaS exist to give portfolio companies
practical tooling and support after the deal closes, when most of the real upside
is actually created. Embedding AI into that post-acquisition support is one concrete
expression of the broader mission: make the businesses Caprae buys measurably better
operators, not just better-financed ones.

## How is Caprae changing the ETA space and broader PE?

Caprae's post-acquisition-first framing pushes against the standard ETA playbook,
where most of the operator's attention goes into the acquisition itself and
integration is treated as an afterthought. Building an internal AI-readiness
practice  screening technical talent through a real product problem instead of a
resume then embedding those hires into portfolio companies  turns "help the
business use AI" from a consulting line item into something the firm does natively,
across its whole portfolio rather than deal-by-deal.
